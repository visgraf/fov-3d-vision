# Demo-Classroom-1 prospective checks

Before execution the new demo source must establish:

1. baseline `cafad30` and branch `demo-classroom-1`;
2. exact Classroom binding `scenes/classroom/classroom_eye.blend`;
3. explicit live-scene preflight before acquisition;
4. oracle assistance is declared, not hidden;
5. Blender reference depth may reject but never replace foreground stereo xyz;
6. foreground/background planning is scene-adaptive and deterministic, not a list of hand-picked Classroom names;
7. exactly one special background scaffold stays outside foreground reconstruction metrics;
8. established Classroom foveated pair/stereo and established FSG/fusion machinery are reused;
9. per-object and global demo guardrails are finite and labeled engineering limits;
10. no autonomous discovery/controller/background-inference claim is made;
11. final foreground-only and composite products plus a visual timeline/movie are required.

The checker also includes mutation controls for truth filling, wrong scene, skipped preflight, non-adaptive background, missing background, unbounded execution, autonomy overclaim, and the wrong baseline.
